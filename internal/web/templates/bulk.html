Bulk

Create many codes at once from a CSV file. You get a ZIP with one image per row and a report.csv that lists any rows that failed.

1. Your CSV
How to lay out the CSV

Name the columns after the content fields. Two extra columns are optional: type (the content type of each row) and name (the image file name).

type,name,url,ssid,password
url,Shop,example.com/shop,,
wifi,Guest Wi-Fi,,Guest,secret

Fields by type:

    {{range .Types}}
  • {{.Name}}: {{range $i, $f := .Fields}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$f.Name}}{{end}}
    • {{end}}
